Shipping Costs Are Separate

!

The membership is for software access only.

The Ship24Go Membership is only for access to the software platform. It does not include shipping labels, courier charges, wallet credits, carrier payments, delivery services, insurance, customs, or third-party logistics services.

These costs are handled separately outside the membership through bank transfer or the customer’s internal Ship24Go wallet.

Transparency

Clear Separation Between Software Membership and Shipping Payments

Ship24Go Membership is a SaaS subscription for software access. Membership payments activate access to the dashboard and member plan features only.

Shipping wallet top-ups, courier charges, shipment purchases, shipping labels, carrier payments, insurance, customs, and delivery services are not processed as part of the membership subscription.

Polar is used only for recurring software membership payments. Shipping-related payments are handled separately outside Polar by bank transfer or through the customer’s internal Ship24Go wallet.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ship24Go Membership a shipping label purchase?

No. Ship24Go Membership is only a software subscription. It gives access to the Ship24Go dashboard and workflow tools. Shipping labels and carrier charges are handled separately.

Does the membership include wallet credits?

No. Wallet top-ups are separate from the membership and are handled outside the recurring subscription.

What do I get with the membership?

You get access to the Ship24Go software platform, including shipment organization, address records, shipment history, rate comparison tools, reports, and member plan features.

How are shipping costs paid?

Shipping costs are handled separately by bank transfer or through the customer’s internal Ship24Go wallet.

Can e-commerce businesses use Ship24Go?

Yes. Ship24Go is designed for merchants, online stores, and businesses that need to organize and manage shipping workflows from one dashboard.
